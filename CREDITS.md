# Photo credits

Photos were cropped and resized for this app.

## Colors game (`colors/images/`)

The 23 Colors photos were prepared by Teacher for the original Colors game. Many of
them are AI-generated or AI-edited images; their individual sources were not recorded.
They were only resized/re-compressed here (max side 800 px, JPEG quality 80).

## Icons and shapes

The app icons (`icons/`) were drawn with Python/PIL and the solid shapes and stars are
inline SVG. Both were made for this project. Letters and 注音 symbols are plain text in
the device's own fonts.
